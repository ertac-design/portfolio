Designing Turkey's largest cinema booking experience.

Overview

Paribu Cineverse is the largest and most prestigious cinema chain in Turkey. A member of the CGV Mars Cinema Group, it reaches millions of moviegoers across the country.

Ticket buying flows

The ticket buying screens were shaped through extensive UX research so people can buy tickets quickly and without friction. The same flow lets them add cinema essentials like popcorn and drinks alongside their tickets.

Kids Family Club Film Festival

For the family section, the goal was a vibrant, engaging experience. The interface is playful but functional: exclusive club benefits stay front and centre while the brand keeps its premium feel.
