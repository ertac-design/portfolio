How I work

  1. 1

    Understand

    I start with the people, the business goal and what already exists, so the work solves the right problem.

  2. 2

    Map the flow

    Flows and wireframes come before pixels. This is where most of the complexity gets removed.

  3. 3

    Design the interface

    Screens are designed as a system, tested early and refined until each one does a single job clearly.

  4. 4

    Refine and hand off

    Visual language, motion and handoff details, so what ships looks and feels like the design.
